## 0. The number that should frame the whole session

Before we split tasks or set KPIs, we need to agree on what the business
actually earns money from today.

| Revenue line | Monthly, actual | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Indwe / Santam sponsorship** (contracted) | **~R208,000** | **~93%** |
| Website paid entries (May–Aug actual) | ~R7,300 | ~3% |
| Membership (48 subs × R149) | ~R7,200 | ~3% |
| Zapper on-course settlements | ~R500–1,000 | <1% |

**Website entries, May → August 2026:** 191 attempts, **157 paid, R29,200 total.**
Tier mix skews cheap — 100 Bronze (R50) against 3 Diamond (R1,000).
Top courses: Victoria 34 · Atlantic Beach 27 · Zimbali 22 · Goose Valley 14 ·
San Lameer 13 · Boschenmeer 12.

**Leads delivered to Indwe:** 584 across May–August (212 · 143 · 188 · 41).

### What this means

The trailing-twelve-month story is R1.5m of entry revenue from 10,000 entries.
That revenue was **promoter-driven** — people standing at holes and in pro shops
selling swings. We have just closed the department that produced it. The digital
channel that remains does R7.3k a month.

So the honest position is: **we are a sponsorship business with a hole-in-one
product attached, and we've just removed the mechanism that produced the
non-sponsorship revenue.**

That is not a criticism of the decision — the handover doc makes the case
plainly, and Liezell's own recommendation was that activations should be
assessed on financial viability before committing resources. It's the right cut.
But it means Q4 has exactly one job: **make the digital channel work, and secure
Indwe 2027.** Everything else is a distraction.

## 3. Final product and UX — the Get Lucky app

The app is now the whole growth story, so scope has to match two founders.

**The brutal input from the funnel data:** of 191 entry attempts, **33 never
paid — a 17% drop-off at the payment step.** Fixing that is worth more than any
new feature. It's also a day of work, not a quarter.

### Proposed v1 scope — ship by end October

Cut to what makes a golfer pay:

1. **Pick course and par-3** — 25 installed courses, then any course
2. **Pick a stake** — R50 → R1,000, the existing ladder
3. **Pay** — one screen, card stored, one-tap repeat (the drop-off fix)
4. **Film and submit** — phone camera, GPS, hole confirmation
5. **Verified / not verified** — status and the payout path
6. **The ace feed** — every ace, everywhere. Cheap to build, and it's the reason
   to open the app when you're not on a course

### Explicitly out of v1

Skins and side games · the Ace Registry as a product · simulator SDK ·
global multi-currency · anything requiring a partner signature we don't have.

The 34-screen master spec in `investgetlucky/GetLucky_App_Requirements.md` is a
good reference and far too big to build. Treat it as the destination, not the
sprint.

### The decision to make in the session

Does Cloud & Things build it, do we contract it, or do we build it here? At
current cash the honest answer is probably **build the PWA in this project and
keep Cloud & Things for when the money is real.**

## 6. Ways of working

Two people, no management layer. The cadence should be light enough to actually
hold.

**Monday, 30 minutes — numbers only.** The ten KPIs on one dashboard. Red, amber,
green. No discussion of anything green.

**Thursday, 60 minutes — one thing.** Whatever the biggest blocker is that week.
Deep work, not status.

**Friday — nothing.** Protect one day of build time each. This is the thing that
always dies first and it's the reason the app isn't built.

**Async by default.** Both of us are copied on almost every email currently. Stop
it — if it's Andrew's domain, Andrew owns the thread. The cc habit made sense
with five people and is pure overhead with two.

**Decisions get written down.** One line in this repo. `HANDOFF.md` in the
investor repo is the proof that this works — every decision for a month is
recoverable. Nothing else we've done has that property.

### The fitness plan

You asked for this in the agenda, so it's in the plan rather than an
afterthought. Two founders carrying a whole company through a hard quarter is
exactly when it goes.

- **Move every morning before email.** 45 minutes. Non-negotiable, and it's the
  only one that matters — the rest is detail.
- **Play golf on Fridays.** We sell golf. Playing it is product research and it's
  the only social exercise on the list.
- **One shared weekly session** — gym, run, whatever. Two founders under pressure
  need unstructured time that isn't a meeting.
- **Track it next to the KPIs.** Same dashboard, same honesty. If it's not
  measured it won't survive October.

## 7. Indwe 2027 and other partners

### Indwe is the company

R3.0m contracted for year two of three, against a total cost base heading below
R150k/month. **Renewing Indwe is close to the entire question of whether Get
Lucky survives 2027.** It should be the first thing on Andrew's list every week.

**What they actually buy:** access to affluent golfers, converted into advisory
leads. Not activations. 584 leads in four months, delivered through an automated
API — that pipeline is the product, and it's the part of the business that
already runs without people.

**What to do about the renewal:**
- Build a **lead quality and conversion report** — not just volume, but what
  Indwe converted. Nobody has asked them for that number. It's the strongest
  renewal argument we have and we don't currently know it.
- Lead volume is the KPI, not activation count. Automating lead capture is
  cheaper than promoters and Indwe doesn't care which produced it.
- The weekly GLG / Indwe / Stratitude call is the renewal process. Treat it that
  way.
- **Clear the TCS.** It's been on the status list for weeks and it's the kind of
  administrative untidiness that costs credibility in a renewal.
